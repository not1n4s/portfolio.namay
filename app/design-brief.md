# Namay Prakash V1 design brief

## Design read
For a thumbnail designer selling sharp editorial instinct to ambitious YouTube creators, the site should feel controlled, kinetic, and immediately credible.

## Concept spine
Namay Prakash’s portfolio treats attention as a visible fluid force. A single chrome frame moves through a pressure chamber, gathering focus until it resolves into a clean portfolio grid.

## Delivery tier
Cinema. The site uses the supplied scroll-scrub engine as its Tier-1 mechanic, with restrained transform motion around the main journey.

## Identity
Personal brand: Namay Prakash.
Brand constraints: free rein for V1 because no existing logo, palette, font, imagery, or client links were supplied.
Logo idea: an FC monogram formed by two crop corners and one flowing cut.
Corner language: soft 16px frames for media, sharp rules for editorial divisions, stadium capsule only for the primary nav CTA.

## Locked palette
Deep ink #10131A, mineral white #F3F0EA, cool slate #9AA0AB, panel ink #171B24, single accent #E05D52.
Defense: the mineral neutral base makes the work feel premium while the muted coral-red accent suggests attention and editorial markup without using familiar neon or amber tech palettes.

## Locked type
Outfit for display and body, with IBM Plex Mono reserved for compact proof labels.
The sans-only pairing keeps the portfolio direct, contemporary, and production-minded.

Animation mode: animated-website

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous 15-second film gives the portfolio a stronger identity and avoids decorative scene switching.
Chapter 1 focal point: a suspended chrome crop frame in dark liquid. Headline: Thumbnails that hold attention. Body: Two years of visual decisions built for the split second before a click. Proof tags: 2 years.
Chapter 2: the crop frame transitions to the action-led train composition. Headline: 25M+ views across the work.
Chapter 3: the crop frame transitions to the vaccine explainer. Headline: Range without losing clarity.
Chapter 4: the crop frame transitions to the heritage story. Headline: Stories built for the first glance.
Chapter 5: the crop frame transitions to the Astana ensemble image. Headline: Every frame earns its place.

## World grammar
One continuous macro product-film world with chrome crop geometry, ink-black liquid glass, mineral reflections, and one muted coral-red current.
Perspective: 50mm macro lens with a slow forward orbit and gentle push-in.
Light direction: large soft source from upper left, narrow rim from lower right.
Surface finish: brushed chrome, smoked glass, viscous lacquer, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
Background behavior: seamless deep ink with generous center-safe negative space for HTML copy.
The camera journey enacts the concept by turning scattered current into one controlled frame.

## Mobile framing
All focal objects stay inside the central 50 percent of the frame. Mobile uses a dedicated 720px encode and center crop with copy anchored below the safest object area.
Delivery budget: desktop clips at or below 32 MiB total, mobile clips at or below 16 MiB total.

## Section plan
1. Fixed single-line navigation with monogram, work anchor, proof anchor, and one capsule CTA.
2. Full-viewport scroll-scrub journey with five semantic chapters over one continuous film.
3. Asymmetric proof band with one large 25M+ figure and a compact two-year context column.
4. Horizontal portfolio rail with six empty media frames, each clearly marked for later thumbnail insertion.
5. Divided process ledger with three unequal rows: frame, sharpen, deliver.
6. Full-width closing statement with a distinct underline-sweep contact CTA.
7. Minimal footer with monogram, role, and availability line.
Layout rule: no layout family repeats consecutively and mobile collapses every multi-column section into a single readable flow.
Eyebrow budget: three maximum across seven sections.

## Asset plan
Storyboard: one 16:9 six-panel continuity board for the liquid-chrome film.
Film: one continuous 16:9 scroll-scrub clip plus desktop and mobile encodes with exact-frame posters.
Hero interaction pair: first-frame and closing-state stills sourced from the encoded film.
Section plates: one liquid-chrome field texture and one quiet grid texture.
Content imagery: portfolio uses deliberate empty thumbnail slots, never generated thumbnails.
Icon set: three custom crop-line icons for concept, composition, and iteration.
Logo family: FC monogram, horizontal wordmark, favicon.
Launch kit: branded 3:2 cover, masked OG image, social metadata.

## CTA inventory
Primary intent label: Start a project.
Nav CTA garment: compact mineral capsule with a coral sliding fill on hover.
Journey CTA garment: text link with a crop-corner bracket that tightens on hover.
Gallery CTA garment: framed insertion slot with an animated focus line, used to invite the real work upload.
Closing CTA garment: oversized text link with a liquid underline sweep and tactile press state.

## Content limits
No invented thumbnails, clients, testimonials, awards, or performance percentages.
Only the supplied facts are used: two years of experience and more than 25 million accumulated views.
Contact links remain neutral V1 anchors until the client provides a real email and social handles.

## Combinatorial reference-board pick
Theme paradigm: Deep Dark, twisted toward mineral ink and smoked chrome rather than graphite tech.
Background character: full-bleed cinematic imagery with solid ambient-depth sections between visual beats.
Typography character: expressive Outfit display with hard grotesk hierarchy.
Hero architecture: massive image-first composition with restrained type anchored off-grid.
Section system: gallery-led cadence.
Signature components: layered image crop frames, oversized metrics strip, vertical rhythm lines, and hover-accordion slices.
Narrative spine: tool and precision instrument.
Second-read moment: one oversized numeral used as structure in the proof band.
Composition anchors: hero bottom-left over image, proof band centered numeral, gallery top-left lead, process off-grid offset, close stacked center.
CTA garments: capsule once in the nav, crop-bracket text link in the journey, framed insertion action in the gallery, and underline sweep in the close.
